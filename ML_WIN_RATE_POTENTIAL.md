## 📊 Win Rate vs. Expected Value (EV)

### The Key Insight: Win Rate Alone Doesn't Matter

**What matters is Expected Value (EV):**

```
EV = (Win Rate × Odds) - 1

Example:
- 55% win rate on 2.0 odds = (0.55 × 2.0) - 1 = 0.10 = 10% edge ✅
- 60% win rate on 1.8 odds = (0.60 × 1.8) - 1 = 0.08 = 8% edge
- 50% win rate on 2.2 odds = (0.50 × 2.2) - 1 = 0.10 = 10% edge ✅
```

**All three have similar profitability despite different win rates!**
