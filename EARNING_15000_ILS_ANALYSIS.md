# 💰 Can You Earn 15,000 ILS/Month from Soccer Betting?

## 🎯 The Reality Check

**Short Answer:** Technically possible, but requires:
- **100,000-200,000 ILS bankroll** (minimum)
- **55-60% win rate** consistently
- **High risk tolerance** (risk of ruin ~10-20%)
- **Professional-level discipline**

---

## 📊 Mathematical Breakdown

### Scenario 1: Conservative Approach (Lower Risk)

**Assumptions:**
- Win Rate: 55% on odds of 2.0 (even money)
- Bet Size: 2% of bankroll per match
- Matches: 30 per month
- Edge: 10% (55% win rate × 2.0 odds = 1.10 expected value)

**Calculation:**
```
Monthly Bet Volume: 30 matches × 2% = 60% of bankroll
Expected Return: 60% × 10% edge = 6% monthly return
Required Bankroll: 15,000 / 0.06 = 250,000 ILS
```

**Result:** Need **250,000 ILS bankroll** to safely earn 15,000/month

---

### Scenario 2: Moderate Approach (Medium Risk)

**Assumptions:**
- Win Rate: 55% on odds of 2.0
- Bet Size: 3% of bankroll per match
- Matches: 40 per month
- Edge: 10%

**Calculation:**
```
Monthly Bet Volume: 40 matches × 3% = 120% of bankroll (reinvesting)
Expected Return: 120% × 10% edge = 12% monthly return
Required Bankroll: 15,000 / 0.12 = 125,000 ILS
```

**Result:** Need **125,000 ILS bankroll** (higher risk)

---

### Scenario 3: Aggressive Approach (High Risk)

**Assumptions:**
- Win Rate: 55% on odds of 2.0
- Bet Size: 5% of bankroll per match
- Matches: 50 per month
- Edge: 10%

**Calculation:**
```
Monthly Bet Volume: 50 matches × 5% = 250% of bankroll (aggressive)
Expected Return: 250% × 10% edge = 25% monthly return
Required Bankroll: 15,000 / 0.25 = 60,000 ILS
```

**Result:** Need **60,000 ILS bankroll** (VERY HIGH RISK - 30%+ risk of ruin)

## ⚠️ Critical Risks

### 1. Risk of Ruin

| Bankroll | Bet Size | Risk of Ruin (55% win rate) |
|----------|----------|------------------------------|
| 60,000 ILS | 5% | **35%** (Very High) |
| 100,000 ILS | 3% | **15%** (High) |
| 150,000 ILS | 2% | **5%** (Moderate) |
| 250,000 ILS | 2% | **<2%** (Low) |

**Risk of Ruin = Probability of losing entire bankroll**

### 2. Drawdown Periods

Even with 55% win rate, you WILL experience:
- **Losing streaks of 5-8 bets** (happens ~10% of months)
- **Monthly losses of 20-30%** during bad periods
- **Psychological pressure** to increase bet sizes (gambler's fallacy)

### 3. Model Degradation

- **Bookmakers adjust odds** when they detect patterns
- **Market efficiency** increases over time
- **Your edge may shrink** from 10% to 5% or less
