**Critical for 70% Win Rate:**

**Only bet when:**
- ✅ Model confidence > 75%
- ✅ Edge > 15%

- ✅ Data quality is excellent (no missing features)
- ✅ Multiple models agree (ensemble consensus)

**Avoid:**

- ❌ Emotional bets or chasing losses
- ❌ Bets where models disagree
- ❌ Markets with high variance (correct score, etc.)
